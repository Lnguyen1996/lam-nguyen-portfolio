# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leaders evaluating Lam Nguyen for full-stack and AI engineering roles. They need to understand his current experience, technical range, system-design judgment, and strongest public work quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Lam as an experienced full-stack and AI engineer who builds dependable distributed systems. Success means visitors can understand his specialty from the opening screen, see his latest Waystar experience, evaluate five representative GitHub projects, and continue to LinkedIn or GitHub without needing an email address.

## Brand Personality

Credible, capable, approachable, and direct. The experience should feel professionally familiar to someone reviewing technical candidates while still being distinctly Lam's own portfolio.

## Anti-references

Avoid copying LinkedIn's interface literally, generic startup language, AI-themed glow effects, gradients, glass treatments, repeated card grids, excessive badges, terminal motifs, oversized decorative typography, and ornamental botanical styling.

## Design Principles

- Lead with Lam's full-stack, AI, and distributed-systems experience.
- Give the latest work experience clear priority before project evidence.
- Present outcomes and engineering decisions before long technology lists.
- Use familiar professional patterns, strong hierarchy, and restrained blue accents.
- Keep every interaction accessible, resilient, and fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA for text contrast, preserve semantic landmarks and keyboard access, provide visible focus states and 44-pixel touch targets, support reduced motion, remain responsive from 320 pixels upward without horizontal overflow, and preserve the essential profile when JavaScript or a portrait is unavailable.
