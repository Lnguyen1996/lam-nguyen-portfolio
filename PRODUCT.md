# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leaders evaluating Lam Nguyen for full-stack engineering roles. They need to understand his role, strengths, judgment, and strongest work quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Lam as a product-minded full-stack engineer through a concise, credible portfolio. Success means visitors can identify his focus and featured work in the first screen, understand the outcomes and engineering decisions behind his projects, and reach him easily.

## Brand Personality

Calm, grounded, and thoughtful. The experience should feel dependable and personally authored, like a carefully edited field journal or independent studio.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic startup language, AI-themed visuals, glass gradients, glow effects, repeated card grids, excessive badges, terminal motifs, decorative complexity, and rustic gardening-brand cues.

## Design Principles

- Make Lam's role and strongest work immediately scannable.
- Present outcomes and engineering decisions before technology lists.
- Use controlled asymmetry and restrained organic details to create a human point of view.
- Keep every interaction accessible, resilient, and fast.
- Prefer clear, useful, and durable choices over clever, flashy, or trendy ones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA for text contrast, preserve semantic landmarks and keyboard access, provide visible focus states and 44-pixel touch targets, support reduced motion, remain responsive from 320 pixels upward without horizontal overflow, and preserve essential identity when JavaScript or the portrait is unavailable.
